# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here.

The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and
this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## 0.4.0 - 2026-10-30

Refs macula-services/mcl-om#42, the migration of the mcl-* services onto
mcl_om 1.6 and macula 18.

- **On mcl_om 1.6 and macula 18, and the service now writes its caller
  decision down.** `mcl_om` moves to `~> 1.6` and `macula` to `~> 18.0`; 18
  still speaks wire 1.0, so this service still speaks to every wire 1.0 box.
  What it brings is a refusal at boot, not a default (macula-io/macula#147):
  a capability that names a handler must name who may call it, and mcl_om
  refuses the boot otherwise. All eleven procedures declare `auth => open`,
  which is the policy they had -- the link ran no gate, the caller is named on
  the call, and a profile crosses a station only as ciphertext. Narrowing
  them to `{callers, members}` is a separate decision and a separate release.
  This service declares no mesh subscription, so the rule's other half --
  `sources` on a subscription -- does not reach it.
- **macula is declared, at 18** (macula-codebase-checks#4, #14). The
  service called macula modules and compiled against `macula_response`
  without declaring the application: it arrived transitively through
  `mcl_om`, so a rebuild took whatever major the newest mcl_om carried.
  `macula` is now a direct dependency. 18 keeps wire 1.0; over 16 it brings
  the two caller rules above and carries 16's revocation bound
  (macula#118), which changes nothing here -- this service gates no overlay
  and issues no endorsement.
- 0.3.0 -> 0.4.0 in the three carriers: the relx release, `mcl_jobs.app.src`
  and the service's `info/0`. The four other applications ship inside this
  release at their own 0.1.0, unchanged since the scaffold.


## 0.3.0 - 2026-10-17

- **Wire 1.0: on macula 15.6 / mcl_om 1.0.** A 15 node neither reads nor handshakes with a 14 node, so this build speaks wire 1.0 only and is held off every live box until the flag day (macula-fleet#27). macula reaches this service through mcl_om and no desk calls it directly, so the move is the pins plus mcl_om 1.0's advertising change.

## 0.2.4 - 2026-10-15

- **Rebuilt on mcl_om 0.42** (mcl-om#26): advertisement renewals move to a 70 s interval under a 280 s TTL (from 30 s / 120 s), so a station sends far fewer renewal frames; the health windows derive from the interval. Carries macula 14.11.0 (mcl_om 0.42's floor).

## 0.2.3 - 2026-10-14

- **Rebuilt on mcl_om 0.40** (mcl-om#25): a lapsed provider delegation claims again and comes back without a restart, and `/health` shows each authorization's `not_after_ms`, degraded as `expiring` with a third or less of its life left.

## [Unreleased]

## [0.2.1] - 2026-10-09

### Changed

- Rebuilt on macula 14.7.0: request admission frees the slot when the reply is
  sent, and caller attribution covers every payload shape (macula#89,
  macula#60).

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-09

### Added

- `describe_profile`, `publish_profile` and `withdraw_profile`, each acting on
  the caller's own profile, with `profile_described_v1`,
  `profile_published_v1` and `profile_withdrawn_v1` and the status flags
  DESCRIBED and PUBLISHED (#2).
- The read side (#3): `project_jobs` projects every profile and organisation
  event into a SQLite read model beside the event store, and `query_jobs`
  answers `get_profile_by_id`, `get_profiles_page`, `get_organisation_by_id`
  and `get_organisations_page`. A profile is seen by the realm when published
  and not archived, and by its owner while not archived; otherwise
  `not_found`.
- `scripts/smoke.sh` covers the new procedures and fails on any reply that was
  not sealed.

### Changed

- Every procedure requires a sealed call (`confidential => required`, with
  `kem_advertise` enabled): a profile crosses a station only as ciphertext.
- Profile text and organisation names are kept in NFC and refuse control and
  invisible format characters (bidi overrides, zero-width characters).

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-08

### Added

- The scaffold, generated by `rebar3 new mcl_service`: an OTP release that
  boots on `mcl_om`, joins the mesh, and answers `/health` on the Unix socket `/run/mcl/health.sock`.
- `mcl_jobs_service`, the six-callback `mcl_om_service` contract, announcing
  four procedures and requesting no topic authority.
- Unit tests asserting the contract's shape, including that the mesh-facing name
  and the reported version match the application's own.
- `Containerfile` building the release in a digest-pinned builder image and
  running it on the matching digest-pinned runtime image.
- `deploy/docker-compose.yml`, the service's own run contract.
- CI: `lint-and-test` (lint, eunit, dialyzer) on every push to `main` and
  every pull request. `build-and-push` to the registry: a push to `main`
  publishes `:latest`, and a `v*` tag publishes its own version and nothing
  else.
- Two CMD divisions on the service's own store, `mcl_jobs_store`:
  `guide_profile_lifecycle` (`initiate_profile`, `archive_profile`) and
  `guide_organisation_lifecycle` (`initiate_organisation`,
  `archive_organisation`). Status is integer bit flags; a dossier is initiated
  once, and archived only when initiated and only once, each rule tested.
- Four mesh procedures, each acting as its caller and replying `{}` or
  `{"reason": ...}`: `mcl-jobs/initiate_profile`, `archive_profile` (the
  caller's own profile), `initiate_organisation` (the caller becomes its
  initiator) and `archive_organisation` (only by its initiator,
  `not_initiator` otherwise).
- `scripts/smoke.sh`: calls a deployed service over the mesh with fresh
  identities and checks every answer, including the refusals only a stored
  event can produce.
- The C4 model in `architecture/`, diagrams in `assets/`.
